How tiers are assigned

Every edge gets a tier from A (strong) to D (limited), with X reserved for genuinely contested questions where the literature itself is split. Tiers are computed from a deterministic scoring function — never by editorial taste — and re-evaluated every time new evidence is added.

TierRequired signalExamples
Strong ≥ 2 high-quality meta-analyses or systematic reviews + total score ≥ 12, with a consistent direction Statins → CVD, Smoking → Lung cancer
Moderate ≥ 1 SR/meta-analysis + ≥ 2 cohorts or RCTs, total score ≥ 7, no major contradictions Resistance training → Depression
Emerging Multiple consistent observational studies or one RCT; promising but not yet replicated Mediterranean diet → Cognitive decline
Contested Literature is genuinely split; meta-analyses disagree Coffee → All-cause mortality (mostly OK, depends on population)
Limited Mechanism only, animal studies, single small trial Fringe supplements with one mouse paper

Full rules live in seed.py + adjudicate.py.

Sources

  • Primary literature: PubMed-indexed papers. Every quantitative evidence row carries a real PMID, automatically verified against PubMed E-utilities — fabricated PMIDs are rejected at validation time.
  • Initial seeding: Claude Sonnet 4.6 with a hard $50 lifetime cost cap, used only for one-off deep research and tier-A/B adjudication. ~$5.50 spent to date.
  • Daily maintenance: local Gemma 4:26b via Ollama — re-tiering, summary refreshing, and ingestion of new evidence rows. No paid API calls in the recurring loop.
  • Retraction watcher: a weekly job hits PubMed for every cited PMID and flags retracted papers. Edges with a retraction in their evidence stack are automatically demoted.

Editorial process

  1. Seed. A topic is identified; Claude pulls the highest-quality literature it can find, attaches PMIDs, drafts a summary, mechanism, caveats, and a tier with reasoning.
  2. Validate. The validator hits PubMed and rejects any row with an unresolvable PMID, missing n_participants, or title mismatch.
  3. Adjudicate. A second Claude pass scores the edge against the deterministic tier rules and flags anything ambiguous.
  4. Maintain. Daily Gemma loop ingests new papers, refreshes prose, and re-runs the tier calculation. The retraction watcher runs weekly.
  5. Audit. Every change is logged in edge_history. See the live changelog below.

Conflicts of interest

  • No supplement, food, or pharmaceutical sponsorships. Health Universe accepts no money or product from anyone whose intervention appears in the graph.
  • No advertising. The site has zero ads and zero ad-tech trackers.
  • Privacy by default. Profile and labs live in a signed cookie + localStorage on your device. Nothing about your stack ever reaches our server unless you subscribe to the digest, in which case we store only the email and tracked-condition slugs.
  • Open source. The codebase is fully public at github.com/prokesmic/HealthUniverse.

Medical advisory board

Health Universe is in open call for clinical reviewers. Until at least one named MD or PharmD is publicly listed here, content should be treated as educational synthesis — not vetted clinical guidance. We're transparent about this rather than pretend a board exists.

  • Cardiometabolic reviewer Open CVD, hypertension, lipidology, T2D edges
  • Geriatric polypharmacy reviewer Open Anticholinergic burden, frailty, drug-disease lists
  • Clinical pharmacist Open Drug × supplement interactions corpus
  • Behavioural / psychiatry reviewer Open Depression, anxiety, sleep, addiction edges

Clinicians: each role is unpaid for now and consists of a one-evening tier audit (30–60 edges) plus quarterly review of promotions/demotions. Email prokesmic@gmail.com with your specialty and credentials.
