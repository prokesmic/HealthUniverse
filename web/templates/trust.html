Evidence audit by tier

Every evidence row with a PMID is checked: we fetch the real title from PubMed and compare it against the edge's factor → outcome with a local embedding model (nomic-embed-text). Cosine similarity ≥ 0.65 = verified; 0.45–0.65 = weak; below 0.45 = flagged. Flagged rows are excluded from the public stack-brief and from tier-aggregation.
