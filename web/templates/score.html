How the score is computed

  • Coverage (50 pts max). For each tracked condition, do you have at least one tier-A protective edge already covered by your stack? %-of-conditions × 50.
  • Stack quality (40 pts max). Sum of tier weights for every protective/neutral factor in your stack (A=4, B=3, C=2, X=1).
  • Interaction penalty. Every active interaction warning subtracts: high=15, moderate=7, low=2.
  • Bands. 0–34 low coverage; 35–69 solid; 70+ strong.
