What stays on your device

  • Your profile — conditions, stack, watchlists. Stored in a signed cookie. The cookie is encrypted with our HMAC secret so nobody else can forge it, but the data lives only on your browser.
  • Lab values — anything entered on /labs and any wearable JSON you upload. Saved in your browser's localStorage. Never uploaded.
  • Diary, protocols — also localStorage.
  • Lab PDFs — when you upload a PDF, the bytes pass through the server for parsing, then are discarded (never written to disk, never logged). Only matched values come back to your browser.

What we store, only if you opt in

Two flows put data on our servers:

  1. Weekly digest signup (/digest or step 3 of /start). We store your email plus a snapshot of your tracked condition slugs (e.g. cvd, t2d) so the Sunday email can be personalised.
  2. Cross-device sync (/sync). We email you a one-time link encoding your profile. The email itself is sent through Resend (or SMTP if configured) and we don't retain the body.

Both are explicit checkbox opt-ins, never on by default.

Lawful basis (GDPR Art. 6 / 9)

  • Profile cookie — Art. 6(1)(b): necessary to deliver the service you signed up for. Not Art. 9 because the cookie itself contains the data and never reaches us.
  • Digest signup — Art. 6(1)(a) explicit consent + Art. 9(2)(a) explicit consent for special-category data (the condition slugs). The checkbox at signup is the consent record.
  • Cross-device sync — Art. 6(1)(b): performance of the service. Email is the recovery anchor.

Retention

  • Profile cookie — 365 days unless you clear it.
  • Sync link tokens — 14 days after generation, then expire automatically (HMAC-signed expiry — no DB cleanup needed).
  • Subscriber list — kept until you unsubscribe; deleted within 24 hours of an unsubscribe action.
  • Lab PDF bytes — discarded immediately after parsing.

What we never collect

  • No third-party trackers, ad networks, or pixels.
  • No fingerprinting, device ID, or cross-site IDs.
  • No analytics service that has access to per-user data.
  • No payment information yet — we'll publish a separate notice when subscriptions launch.

Your rights

Under GDPR (and equivalent regimes — CCPA, UK GDPR), you can:

  • Access — download everything we know about you as JSON from /data-export.
  • Erase — clear the cookie in your browser to delete the profile; unsubscribe below to remove the email + condition snapshot.
  • Object / restrict / port — email the contact below.
  • Complain — to your national supervisory authority if you believe processing is unlawful.
