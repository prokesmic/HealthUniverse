Apple Health (export.xml or .zip) · Oura (trends.csv) · Garmin (CSV/zip)
Whoop (CSV) · Polar Flow (CSV) · Strava (activities.csv)

Apple Health

  1. On your iPhone, open the Health app.
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right).
  3. Scroll down → Export All Health Data → Export.
  4. Share the zip to your laptop (AirDrop / iCloud Drive / email).
  5. Drag the export.zip or extracted export.xml above.
~5-30 MB · gives daily HRV, RHR, sleep, steps

Oura Ring

  1. Open cloud.ouraring.com in a browser.
  2. Account → Export Data → pick a time range.
  3. Save the trends.csv that downloads.
  4. Drag it above.
CSV · gives sleep, HRV (rMSSD), readiness, body temp

Garmin

  1. Open Garmin Data Management.
  2. Click Export Your Data → wait for the email (typically < 1 hr).
  3. Download the .zip.
  4. Drag the .zip above — we'll pick out the sleep + HRV files.
.zip · gives sleep, HRV (overnight avg), RHR, training load

Whoop

  1. Open the Whoop app → More tab.
  2. Membership → Export My Data → choose what + email it.
  3. Download the CSV(s) the email links to.
  4. Drag the physiological_cycles.csv above.
CSV · gives recovery %, HRV, RHR, strain

Polar Flow

  1. Sign in at flow.polar.com.
  2. Profile icon → Account → Export Data.
  3. Pick CSV format, time range, download.
  4. Drag it above.
CSV · gives sleep, HRV, recovery, training

Strava

  1. Sign in at strava.com → Account.
  2. Scroll to Download or Delete Your Account → Get Started → Request Your Archive.
  3. Wait for the email (can be hours), download the .zip.
  4. Drag activities.csv above.
CSV inside zip · gives weekly active minutes, HR averages

Files are parsed in your browser. The aggregated daily summaries (sleep minutes, HRV, RHR) are stored in your local profile and — only if you've signed in & enabled encrypted sync — backed up to your account in AES-encrypted form. Raw files are never uploaded.
