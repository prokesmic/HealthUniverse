# Claude Brief: Myths and Contested Claims

Route:
- `/myths`

Files:
- `web/templates/myths.html`
- `web/static/style.css`

Goal:
- Make `/myths` feel like a sharp, editorially serious page about overturned and contested health ideas.

Current function to preserve:
- shows contested or deprecated edges
- displays reason when available
- links to underlying edge

Design direction:
- intellectually honest
- slightly sharper tone than the rest of the app
- should feel like “what the field got wrong or still argues about”

What to improve:
- stronger framing for contested vs deprecated
- cards should communicate disagreement or reversal clearly
- reasons for change should read as a meaningful part of the card

Acceptance criteria:
- page has a distinct editorial identity
- contested/deprecated states are immediately legible
- cards remain clean and trustworthy
